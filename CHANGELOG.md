### Added

- GPU geodesic ray tracer for a binary black hole with accretion disks and a procedural sky.
- Post pass with bloom, halation, anamorphic streak, AgX tonemap, grade and grain.
- Loop timeline: inspiral, merger at 41 s, ringdown, light wash, seamless cut at 58.5 s.
- `scripts/render` and `scripts/encode`; SvelteKit page that plays the loop fullscreen.
